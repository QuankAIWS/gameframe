# Monster Master RPG Lore Tone and Agent Realization

## Authority

This addendum refines the tone language in Decision 1 of `monster-master-rpg-lore-and-story.md` without replacing the rest of the accepted lore decision log.

Where Decision 1 describes comedy as primarily dry or cautions against slapstick, this addendum and `monster-master-rpg-current-creative-direction.md` control.

## World posture

Monster Master is a fantasy-adventure world for adult players. It should feel socially and morally varied rather than sanitized, relentlessly grim, or built to teach one approved lesson.

The world contains:

- decent and heroic people;
- ordinary people trying to get through the day;
- selfish, reckless, foolish, compromised, corrupt, and dangerous people;
- competent institutions and failing institutions;
- sincere relationships and transactional relationships;
- lawful work, informal work, legal gray areas, and organized crime;
- monsters that are companions, workers, citizens, hazards, predators, victims, opportunists, and people in their own right according to intelligence and local law.

Players may choose to help, protect, investigate, negotiate, follow procedure, bend rules, take advantage, commit crimes, withdraw, refuse responsibility, or attempt something unexpected when the fiction permits it.

No posture is required merely because the starter uses a certification circuit. Heroic play should be rewarding. Rule-bending or opportunistic play should create real possibilities and real exposure. The DM should not preach, sanitize consequences, or treat players like children.

## Comedy and seriousness

Monster Master supports:

- dry humor;
- broad situational comedy;
- slapstick and physical comedy;
- absurd professional titles, customs, organizations, and terminology;
- selective jokes approaching meme energy;
- sincere emotional scenes;
- adventure and tactical danger;
- funny horror;
- bounded genuine horror.

The setting takes continuity and consequences seriously, but it does not protect its dignity.

A respected expert may have a ridiculous title. A frightening monster may create an absurd logistical problem. A foolish person may become heroic. A decent person may make a cowardly choice. A criminal may have an understandable motive without becoming innocent. A lawful authority may be useful without becoming morally perfect.

Avoid:

- constant jokes;
- empty reference spam;
- self-aware parody that makes the world feel fake;
- solemn exposition as the default voice;
- corporate training-simulation dialogue;
- morality lectures disguised as NPC reactions;
- grimness that prevents relief, wonder, or fun.

## Accepted professional title — the Master Baiter

The setting includes a highly accomplished monster-luring specialist commonly called **the Master Baiter**.

The title is funny. The expertise is real.

The character understands bait, scent, sound, habitat, feeding behavior, migration, territorial displays, and nonviolent methods of moving monsters. Professionals treat the title seriously. Other people may fail to do so.

The character must remain useful and respected after the joke lands. He is not automatically inserted into every story and is not required in the starter scenario unless the plot package gives him a functional role.

## Plot-agent output is story potential

The future plot agent should not write a rigid adventure script.

It should produce a committed scenario package containing:

- hidden truth and causal history;
- actors with goals, fears, leverage, secrets, and behavioral limits;
- world and starting state;
- functional spine requirements;
- early affordances;
- clues and redundant evidence relationships;
- pressure and escalation rules;
- state-aware event material;
- tactical thresholds and objectives;
- consequence and resolution envelopes;
- semantic asset roles;
- forbidden retcons and realization constraints.

The package describes what is true, what is in motion, and what material is available. It does not prescribe numbered scenes.

## DM-agent realization is the live story

The DM agent uses the language model to turn the package into play around the actual group.

The DM owns:

- narration and sensory description;
- dialogue and NPC performance;
- pacing and event timing;
- interpretation of freeform player intent;
- state-compatible event selection;
- adaptation to relationships, skills, creatures, locations, and prior choices;
- secondary detail that does not alter committed truth;
- GameFrame presentation and Arena Battles requests through the defined contracts.

The DM must not:

- replace the cause or culprit;
- run the package as a mandatory scene sequence;
- invent decisive evidence only to rescue pacing;
- move evidence merely because the players guessed correctly or incorrectly;
- force heroism, criminality, combat, or procedural compliance;
- treat suggested approaches as the full action space;
- expose runtime-only truth;
- reverse established outcomes.

The LLM is not decorative prose generation. Its purpose is to adapt the same causal package into materially different, coherent stories for different players.

## Functional spine

A scenario spine is a causal and functional backbone.

For the starter, the spine should ensure that play can:

- establish why the party is together;
- reach meaningful agency quickly;
- provide several credible kinds of action;
- introduce the incident and its pressures;
- allow investigation, social action, creature care, practical work, deception, force, avoidance, and freeform alternatives where credible;
- support noncombat and objective-driven tactical resolution;
- conclude the immediate incident;
- retain only an optional continuation hook.

These functions may occur in different orders, combine, or emerge through player-created situations.

## Event pools

An event pool is authored dramatic material with eligibility and consequences.

An event may create:

- a public signal;
- an opportunity;
- pressure;
- evidence access;
- an NPC reaction;
- a creature behavior change;
- a practical problem;
- a temptation;
- a legal or social complication;
- a tactical threshold;
- an aftermath consequence.

The DM selects and realizes only state-compatible events. An event is not final prose and is not automatically mandatory.

## Consequence posture

Consequences should follow from the world rather than from an invisible morality score.

Relevant consequences include:

- safety and injury;
- creature fear, health, trust, refusal, or placement;
- evidence condition;
- legal exposure;
- certification and professional reputation;
- NPC trust, hostility, debt, or leverage;
- money, supplies, cube condition, and equipment;
- route or infrastructure damage;
- future event eligibility;
- optional continuation opportunities.

A heroic choice can fail. A selfish choice can work. A lawful choice can be expensive. An illegal shortcut can be useful. The DM should represent those outcomes honestly rather than correcting them toward a preferred lesson.

## Asset implication

Prepared art should form a reusable visual vocabulary for the scenario package:

- locations with composable states;
- recurring actors with useful expression ranges;
- modular evidence, equipment, cargo, and environmental props;
- creature states and tactical derivatives;
- private observation and investigation treatments;
- objective markers and aftermath consequence summaries.

Do not require unique art for every event realization. The same prepared assets should support different stories produced from the same package.

## Validation

The first package should remain coherent when players are:

- straightforward and heroic;
- cautious and procedural;
- opportunistic or willing to bend rules;
- aggressive investigators;
- chaotic, avoidant, or unwilling to accept the apparent assignment.

The hidden truth must survive all of them. The resulting stories should differ because the DM adapts to player action, not because the runtime secretly replaces the plot.

## Governing rule

> Monster Master supplies a coherent adult world and committed causal package; the DM model supplies the live story, with broad player freedom, natural consequences, and no requirement to behave like either ideal heroes or constant criminals.
