# Monster Master RPG Lore Tone and Agent Realization

The decisions previously maintained in this addendum are now reconciled into [`monster-master-rpg-canonical-baseline.md`](monster-master-rpg-canonical-baseline.md) and [`monster-master-rpg-current-creative-direction.md`](monster-master-rpg-current-creative-direction.md).

Use the canonical baseline as the authority for:

- broad adult fantasy-adventure tone;
- slapstick, absurd professional culture, funny horror, and bounded genuine horror;
- player freedom without forced heroism or forced criminality;
- several approved plot families with no canonical incident;
- one committed package per run;
- the campaign-compiler/plot-agent and live-DM split;
- natural consequences instead of morality lectures;
- reusable assets rather than fixture-specific production.

Older language in `monster-master-rpg-lore-and-story.md` that describes comedy as primarily dry or broadly cautions against slapstick is superseded by the canonical baseline.

This file remains only as a compatibility pointer for existing links. Do not add new decisions here.
