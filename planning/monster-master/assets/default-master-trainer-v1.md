# Default Human Master Trainer v1

## Status

Approved source specification. No runtime derivative is accepted yet.

## Identity

- Family: `trainer`
- Continuity ID: `trainer-default-master-v1`
- Semantic role: initial human trainer/player-character presentation
- Explicit exclusion: not a monster species and not part of the creature atlas

The default trainer is a prepared reference character for the first pack and deterministic fixtures. Later customization may replace or layer appearance, clothing, accessories, portraits, and other presentation traits through separate reviewed work.

## Battlefield source requirements

- clearly human adult trainer;
- elevated three-quarter view compatible with the 72×36 pseudo-isometric battlefield;
- full body visible with one stable ground-contact region;
- readable at roughly 80–120 CSS pixels tall;
- practical field clothing and restrained command equipment;
- calm command or call-in pose rather than a creature attack pose;
- mirror-safe design unless a directional family is explicitly authored;
- no monster anatomy, transformation state, giant armor shell, franchise-derived costume, text, watermark, scenery, or large baked shadow;
- transparent retained source master with enough padding for head, hands, clothing, and feet.

## Visual direction

Use an illustrated late-1990s/early-2000s creature-battling PC-art sensibility: readable silhouette, controlled ink edges, painted or cel-and-gouache surface, restrained palette, clear value groups, and limited microdetail. Avoid glossy toy-like 3D rendering and uncontrolled armor or particle clutter.

## Planned outputs

After source approval:

- 512-pixel battlefield derivative;
- 256-pixel compact battlefield derivative;
- silhouette fallback;
- measured anchor and scale metadata;
- procedural shadow profile.

Dialogue and roster portraits are authored as separate presentation sources rather than assumed to be automatic crops from the battlefield cutout.

## Acceptance

Reject or rework any source that reads as a monster, hides or floats the feet, fails at battlefield scale, cannot be mirrored safely, includes fake transparency or scenery, or resembles protected franchise character design too closely.

Exact anchors and scale values are measured from the cleaned accepted source and proven in the live renderer.
