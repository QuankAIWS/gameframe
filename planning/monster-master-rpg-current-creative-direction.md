# Monster Master RPG Current Creative Direction

## Purpose

This document corrects two current-phase misunderstandings:

1. the present asset-production work is performed directly by the owner and the assisting creative agent, not by RPG GM Runtime, Cloudflare, or an automated generation service;
2. Monster Master is a comedic fantasy adventure with broad tonal range, not a mostly serious professional mystery with occasional dry jokes.

Where earlier planning language is more restrictive, this document controls the current creative direction.

## Current asset-production mode

The full asset register remains useful and should be retained. It is an inventory, prioritization tool, and provenance ledger for work that **we create, edit, review, and integrate directly** during the current phase.

Current production may include:

- image generation performed interactively during development;
- editing and cleaning generated or existing images;
- hand-authored interface elements and icons;
- deterministic crops, masks, derivatives, and asset manifests;
- reuse and improvement of existing Arena Battles assets;
- manual review through local GameFrame screenshots and playable scenes.

The following are future capabilities, not current dependencies or implementation targets:

- Cloudflare-managed image generation;
- RPG GM Runtime creating assets;
- live campaign-time generation queues;
- automatic prompt compilation and provider routing;
- runtime asset replacement or generation orchestration.

The asset register may record fields needed by a future automated pipeline, but those fields must not be mistaken for work that must be implemented before the starter can be authored and played.

## Tone correction

Monster Master should be a good-time fantasy adventure capable of moving among:

- slapstick and physical comedy;
- absurd professional titles, names, institutions, and customs;
- jokes that approach meme energy without making every scene a reference or punchline;
- sincere character relationships;
- unsettling monster behavior;
- funny horror;
- genuine dark horror;
- exciting tactical danger;
- moments of wonder and emotional weight.

The setting takes its own world seriously enough for consequences and continuity to matter, but it does not protect its dignity. Important, competent, respected people may have ridiculous names or titles. A frightening creature encounter may contain an absurd practical problem. A ridiculous character may become genuinely heroic. A funny premise may reveal real danger.

Slapstick is allowed and sometimes desirable. Borderline-meme concepts are allowed when they arise naturally from the setting and are not repeated until they become noise. The restriction is against **constant joke density**, empty reference spam, and comedy that prevents the audience from caring—not against broad comedy itself.

## Accepted character concept — the Master Baiter

The setting includes a highly accomplished and widely respected monster-luring specialist commonly known as **the Master Baiter**.

The title is funny for the obvious reason, but the character is not merely a disposable joke. He is exceptionally knowledgeable about bait, scent, sound, habitat, migration, feeding behavior, territorial displays, and nonviolent methods of drawing monsters toward or away from a location.

People who know the profession treat the title and his expertise with complete seriousness. Less disciplined people struggle to do so. He may be pompous, dignified, eccentric, intimidating, warm, or some combination, but he must be legitimately excellent at his work.

The joke should usually be allowed to land without every character explaining it. Repeated dialogue may exploit the title occasionally, but the character must remain useful, memorable, and respected after the first laugh.

## One-shot tonal application

The starter certification outing should not play like a procedural crime drama with monsters added.

Its active incident can still contain illegal transport, frightened creatures, danger, evidence, and consequences, but the route to those stakes should include colorful personalities, ridiculous field practices, visual comedy, creature behavior, professional friction, and opportunities for player-created chaos.

The experience may become frightening around the culvert or during containment. That horror should contrast with—not erase—the comedic adventure around it. The desired result is not comedy pasted onto horror or horror interrupting a comedy campaign; it is a world capable of both without changing identities.

## Creative test

A Monster Master scene is on target when:

- the players can laugh without being told the world is a parody;
- the situation remains playable after the joke lands;
- capable characters are allowed to be ridiculous and ridiculous characters are allowed to be capable;
- horror can become genuinely uncomfortable for a bounded stretch;
- the session returns to adventure, camaraderie, and fun rather than remaining grim;
- the setting produces memorable stories instead of a continuous stream of disconnected gags.

## Governing rule

> Build a coherent fantasy world with enough confidence to be ridiculous, enough danger to become frightening, and enough heart that both modes matter.
