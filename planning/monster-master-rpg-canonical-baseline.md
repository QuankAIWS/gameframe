# Monster Master RPG Canonical Baseline

## Purpose

This is the required starting point for Monster Master work. The broader RPG platform architecture is controlled by `shared/rpg-agent-architecture-and-campaign-package.md`, and implementation order is controlled by `shared/rpg-platform-roadmap.md`.

Monster Master is the first handcrafted campaign built for that reusable platform. It is not the platform architecture and it is not a special Dungeon Master implementation.

## Platform context

The RPG platform has two specialized agents.

### Campaign Architect

The Campaign Architect creates complete, validated CampaignPackages from player concepts, owner-authored specifications, structured sheets, later GameFrame or Discord interviews, prepared settings, or imported packages.

Older terms such as campaign compiler, plot agent, and campaign-generation agent refer to responsibilities inside the Campaign Architect. They do not define additional agents.

The Campaign Architect owns campaign construction: setting, campaign bible, actors, motives, locations, campaign spine, plot structure, clue graph, event pools, escalation, resolution, mechanics and presentation requirements, provenance, validation, and package output.

### Dungeon Master

The Dungeon Master consumes one committed CampaignPackage and the durable campaign journal. It conducts live play through narration, NPC performance, freeform intent interpretation, pacing, eligible events, compatible improvisation, consequences, checks, and tactical requests.

The Dungeon Master may fill local gaps but may not replace campaign truth, responsible actors, evidence logic, established facts, prior consequences, tactical outcomes, or established NPC identities.

There is no separate intro agent. The opening is the first Dungeon Master turn after package commitment.

## CampaignPackage rule

Handcrafted and generated campaigns use the same package contract, validation, persistence, commitment, and Dungeon Master interface.

The Dungeon Master must not care whether a package was:

- handcrafted by the team;
- generated by the Campaign Architect;
- assembled through a structured form;
- produced through a Discord interview;
- imported and migrated.

Package origin is provenance, not a separate runtime mode.

## Monster Master role

Monster Master is:

1. the first playable campaign;
2. the handcrafted gold-standard CampaignPackage;
3. the quality bar for future Campaign Architect output;
4. the principal Dungeon Master behavior and continuity fixture;
5. the proving ground for GameFrame presentation and Arena Battles integration.

The team manually authors everything a mature Campaign Architect should eventually produce:

- campaign setting and operating rules;
- campaign bible and continuity invariants;
- packages, plot structures, clues, event pools, escalation, and resolutions;
- actors, NPC roles, locations, factions, and relationships;
- tactical opportunities and consequence rules;
- semantic asset manifest, prepared assets, and fallbacks;
- deterministic and scripted test fixtures.

The existing plot and NPC catalogs are source material. They are not complete executable campaigns until converted into validated CampaignPackages.

## Reconciled Monster Master decisions

### Tone and agency

Monster Master is an adult fantasy-adventure setting supporting dry humor, broad comedy, slapstick, absurd professional culture, selective meme-adjacent concepts, sincere relationships, tactical danger, funny horror, and bounded genuine horror.

Heroic and lawful play is valid but not mandatory. Practical, selfish, opportunistic, illegal, reckless, avoidant, and unexpected actions may be credible. Consequences come through the world, not invisible morality correction.

### Starter structure

The certification circuit is a reusable chassis, not a canonical incident or mandatory scene sequence.

No deterministic fixture is product canon. A fixture does not control default story, lore priority, NPC planning, or asset production.

The first implementation goal is one complete executable Monster Master CampaignPackage. Additional plot families follow after that package and the Dungeon Master loop work.

### NPCs

Prepared roles provide recurring anchors and asset coverage, but the cast is not closed. The Dungeon Master may create plausible incidental NPCs with stable IDs, appearance, facts, promises, debts, injuries, and relationships.

An incidental NPC may not retroactively become a committed culprit, decisive witness, clue owner, or secret authority unless the CampaignPackage explicitly left that role open.

### Monster Master identity

The Master is a human trainer/player character. Trainers and monsters are separate rules and asset families. Compatibility-era IDs do not define monster species.

### Assets

The CampaignPackage declares semantic asset requirements. GameFrame resolves, composes, generates, validates, stores, versions, and delivers assets.

The Campaign Architect eventually determines the campaign's required media coverage. The Dungeon Master uses accepted identities during play and does not redesign the asset pack every turn.

Cloudflare-backed image generation is a later campaign-preparation capability, not a prerequisite for campaign logic or Dungeon Master testing.

## Current implementation order

1. implement the executable CampaignPackage schema, validation, persistence, and commitment path;
2. convert Monster Master source material into one complete handcrafted gold-standard package;
3. make the Dungeon Master consume committed package context for opening and later turns;
4. build multi-turn machine-play tests with mock providers and scripted players;
5. implement only the checks, event progression, clue state, NPC continuity, and tactical handoff required by the package;
6. make the complete Monster Master campaign playable through GameFrame;
7. implement the Campaign Architect against the proven package contract;
8. prove at least one materially different generated bespoke campaign through the same Dungeon Master;
9. add richer intake, Discord interviews, and media generation;
10. broaden mechanics, campaign scope, deployment polish, and scale only after the core system works.

## Required test posture

The platform must distinguish:

- catalog-shape tests;
- package validation tests;
- Dungeon Master behavior tests;
- transport and persistence tests;
- cross-repository integration tests;
- player-interface tests;
- VM and media-provider canaries.

No transport test, catalog assertion, or canned two-turn opening may be described as a working campaign.

The first serious campaign harness must run multiple turns, use scripted player behavior, test early correct guesses and missed clues, create and revisit an incidental NPC, assert that package truth never changes, test secret leakage, reach a valid resolution or tactical threshold, and survive retry and restart.

## Superseded material

Do not follow any document or implementation that:

- sends a raw premise directly to the Dungeon Master and calls that campaign creation;
- treats the Dungeon Master as responsible for inventing the campaign foundation;
- creates separate Monster Master and generic Dungeon Master paths;
- creates a third intro agent;
- treats the current catalogs as complete executable campaigns;
- makes one incident or deterministic fixture canonical;
- makes generated media a requirement for campaign logic testing;
- prioritizes deployment hardening over the executable package and agent loop without a concrete blocker;
- includes Hyperbolic Time Cube or confirmed Class Five material in the starter.

## Governing rule

> Handcraft Monster Master as the gold-standard CampaignPackage, prove one Dungeon Master can run it coherently, then build the Campaign Architect to create new packages of the same quality.
