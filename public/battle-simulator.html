SHARED WITH ROLE-PLAYING GAMES

One game family, two ways to play

Shared RulesetThe RPG and Battle Simulator use the same legal actions, initiative, conditions, resources, character controls, and tactical outcomes.
Battle PacksA combat-capable RPG family can expose simulator-safe characters, enemies, equipment, map themes, objectives, bots, and presets without copying its rules.
Shared AssetsCharacters, creatures, terrain, props, effects, and world kits created for a campaign can be reused for standalone battles.
Spoiler ControlCampaign secrets and unrevealed content stay hidden unless the Battle Pack explicitly exposes or unlocks them.
Generated FamiliesWhen the Campaign Architect eventually creates a new combat-capable RPG family, it can produce Battle Simulator content alongside the campaign.
Same Tactical CoreBattle Simulator starts from scenario setup; campaign combat starts where the party is already standing. The actual tactical rules remain shared.
