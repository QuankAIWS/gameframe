UNDER THE GAMES

One reusable RPG engine

Persistent WorldsGenerated or handcrafted maps remain the same places when you return.
Game MasterA real GM channel handles rulings, knowledge, consequences, and unanticipated actions.
Characters & PartiesRulesets define characters, controlled creatures, classes, resources, and party authority.
Tactical ModeInitiative turns on in the map you are already standing in; combat does not load a separate battlefield.
Generated WorldsCampaign semantics become durable playable scenes through reusable world kits and materialization.
Ruleset SupportMonster Master is the first ruleset/content family, not the engine itself.
